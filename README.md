# Customer Segmentation for Targeted Marketing Campaigns

## Project Overview

This project focuses on customer segmentation for **FinTech Solutions S.p.A.**, a company operating in the financial services sector.

The objective is to develop a Machine Learning clustering model able to group credit card customers into homogeneous segments based on their spending behavior, payment habits, credit card usage, cash advance activity, credit limit, and customer tenure.

The final goal is to support targeted marketing campaigns by identifying customer groups with similar behaviors and designing specific marketing strategies for each segment.

## Project Brief

The project brief required the development of a customer segmentation model for a financial services company.

FinTech Solutions S.p.A. wants to promote its credit card products more effectively by segmenting customers into different groups. This segmentation allows the company to personalize marketing campaigns, improve customer experience, optimize marketing resources, and identify business growth opportunities.

The main phases required by the brief are:

1. Exploratory Data Analysis
2. Data preprocessing
3. Customer segmentation through clustering
4. Cluster interpretation
5. Development of targeted marketing strategies
